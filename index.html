5 · Estimate the value

Imagine buying today, collecting your slice of cash each year, then selling at the end of the forecast. What is all of that worth today? Do it for three futures.

Owner margin = profit margin after all costs including staff paid in shares. The official (GAAP) operating margin in step 3 already counts stock pay as a cost, so it is a good guide. If you use a company's "adjusted" operating margin instead, subtract stock pay as % of sales.
